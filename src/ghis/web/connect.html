ghis See a demo

Your code,
told beautifully.

ghis reads your commits, pull requests and reviews from GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg or any git host, and turns them into an animated story of everything you've built.

    One HTTPS clone URL per line, on any host git can clone from.
    How do I get a token?
      Commits are matched by author email. Your account's emails are added automatically.
      One per line. Leave empty to search your whole home folder. Checkouts found are read in place instead of being cloned, which is faster and covers repositories that are not online.

      Your token is used once, held in memory only and never written to disk. Clones are deleted when the story is ready.

      Already ran the CLI? Drop a history.json here or .

      How it works

      1. Pick where your code livesGitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, or any git host by URL.
      2. Paste an access tokenghis uses it only to read your repositories, pull requests and reviews through the host's API.
      3. ghis clones and readsEvery repository you committed to, matched by your author email. Large accounts take a few minutes.
      4. Enjoy your storyFilter by year, hover anything for details, download the data or print it as a PDF.
      Every commit, every repo

      Real clones, real diffs: lines, languages, files and your biggest moments.

      Your rhythm

      A day-by-day calendar, streaks and the hours you actually write code.

      Collaboration

      Pull and merge requests, time to merge, reviews and approvals. On GitLab and Codeberg, comments too.
